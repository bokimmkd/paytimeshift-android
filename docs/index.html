    A FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

    Before your first shift.

    Do I need an account?

    You can plan jobs and shifts locally without signing in. An account is needed for Premium cloud backup and restore.

    Are the earnings my official payslip?

    PTS shows estimates based on the shifts and rules you enter. It does not automatically calculate taxes or create an official payslip. Monthly deductions are entered manually.

    Can a colleague see my pay?

    A colleague schedule export includes dates, shift times, breaks and day types. It excludes your rates, income, work costs, bonuses and private notes. Review an imported schedule before saving it to your chosen job.

    Which languages are available in the app?

    English, Macedonian, German, Italian, Spanish, French, Serbian, Portuguese (Brazil) and Greek. English is the default.

    Can I move my data to another phone?

    Save and restore a manual backup file in Settings. Premium also offers cloud backup and restore after signing in. Review the restore choice before replacing local data.
